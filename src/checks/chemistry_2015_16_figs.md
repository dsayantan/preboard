# Figures — Chemistry pre-board 2015-16 (src/figs/chemistry_2015_16_figs.py)

All rendered with `preview.py` and at 2x zoom, and looked at. The paper prints no diagrams (only two tables,
reproduced as HTML tables), so every figure is an answer figure.

- fig_pt_cis_trans (1(d)(iii)): square-planar Pt, four ligands at 90°; cis has the two Cl adjacent (N, E), trans opposite (N, S). Labels clear of bonds.
- fig_co_en2cl2_optical (5(b)): octahedron drawn with front/back wedge; Cl at up and front (cis, 90° apart); each en spans two cis positions (L–D and R–back). Isomer II is generated by negating every x offset of isomer I, so it is a true mirror image; checked visually that neither en arc crosses a bond.
- fig_fecn6_vbt (5(c)): Fe 3d6 4s2 → Fe3+ 3d5 (five singles) → in [Fe(CN)6]3−: 3d ↑↓ ↑↓ ↑ + two ligand pairs, 4s and three 4p ligand pairs; bracket spans exactly the six d2sp3 orbitals; 1 unpaired, μ = √3 = 1.73 BM.
- fig_azeotropes (3(c)): T–x curves computed (not freehand): liquid and vapour curves share both pure-component end points and touch with a common horizontal tangent at the azeotrope; vapour (dashed) lies ABOVE liquid everywhere in both panels; min-b.p. panel labelled +ve deviation (ethanol–water 351 K), max-b.p. panel −ve deviation (HNO3–water 393.5 K).
- fig_ag_conc_cell (4(b)): left Ag in 0.001 M = anode (−), right Ag in 0.1 M = cathode (+), KNO3 salt bridge (not KCl, which would precipitate AgCl), electron arrows in the external wire left → right; half-equations under each beaker match.
- fig_ester_mech (9(c)(i)): protonation of C=O (curly arrow from carbonyl O to H+), ethanol O lone pair → C+, tetrahedral intermediate with O+(H)C2H5, proton moved to OH (–OH2+), curly arrows for C–OH2+ cleavage and O lone pair forming C=O, then loss of H+. Every carbon has four bonds; the ester's –OC2H5 (from the alcohol) highlighted.
- fig_glucose_structures (10(c)(iii)): Fischer D-glucose with OH right, left, right, right on C2–C5 (D because C5–OH right). Haworth: ring O back-right, C1 right; groups right in Fischer drawn down (C2-OH down, C3-OH up, C4-OH down), CH2OH up on C5; α has C1-OH down, β up. Front edges heavy.

Review fixes (independent review): fig_azeotropes — legend moved between the panels (canvas 640→700 wide, right panel shifted) because "vapour" overlapped the B end-label; HNO₃ subscript. fig_ester_mech — the C–OH₂⁺ cleavage arrow now runs from the bond to the O and no longer covers the + charge. fig_ag_conc_cell — KNO₃ subscript.
