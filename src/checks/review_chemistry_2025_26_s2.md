## Points settled without change

- **Filter (both sets): no exclusions, confirmed.** Carbon dating — first-order kinetics problem ("Problems based on first order rate equation and half-life period"); vitamins — "Vitamins A, B, C, D, E and K … deficiency"; lead accumulator — "Dry cell and lead accumulator"; HI/red P — "Reduction … (Clemmensen's, Wolff-Kishner, Red phosphorus and HI)"; HCHO + NH₃ (urotropine) — "Reactions with ammonia and its derivatives"; hydroboration — "indirect hydration, hydroboration" under alcohol preparation. Nucleic acid bases, isoelectric point, peptide bond, Sandmeyer, Hofmann, carbylamine, Fittig, Dow, Williamson, Lucas, spectrochemical series, VBT/CFT all listed.
- **S1 Q7** — paper prints +0.88 V (verified in the XML); kept with the existing note (+0.80 V tabulated); answers (Ag⁺ oxidises I⁻, E° 0.34 V; reaction II spontaneous, +1.93 V; I is −1.32 V) recomputed and correct.
- **S1 Q12 OR** — the embedded scheme image reads [A] →(conc. HNO₃/conc. H₂SO₄, Δ) nitrobenzene →[B] aniline →(Ac₂O) [C] ↓(HNO₃) [D] →(NaNO₂/HCl, 0–5 °C) [E] →(HBF₄; NaNO₂, Cu) [F]. The standard NCERT route (acetylation to protect –NH₂, p-nitration, hydrolysis, diazotisation, fluoroborate + NaNO₂/Cu → –NO₂) gives A benzene, B Sn/HCl, C acetanilide, D 4-nitroaniline (hydrolysis step implied; nitration product 4-nitroacetanilide is shown), E 4-nitrobenzenediazonium chloride, F 1,4-dinitrobenzene — the writer's identification is correct and the note about the missing hydrolysis step is appropriate. All six drawn structures checked: para substitution throughout, correct substituents.
- **S1 Q17** — table heads "Rate of disappearance of [N₂O₅]", so rate = −d[N₂O₅]/dt = k[N₂O₅] and k = rate/[N₂O₅] ≈ 2.96 × 10⁻² min⁻¹ is the answer asked; the per-mole-of-reaction k′ = 1.48 × 10⁻² min⁻¹ is correctly kept as a note only. Order ratios 1.04 and 1.08 recomputed.
- **S1 Q20(iii)** — A (Mohr's salt, double salt, free ions) red; B complex salt, Fe³⁺ locked in [Fe(C₂O₄)₃]³⁻. The answer correctly attributes the red colour to Fe³⁺ (always present from aerial oxidation of Fe²⁺) forming [Fe(SCN)]²⁺. Kept.
- **S2 1(B)(ii)** — CO (strongest field ligand); note that CO is neutral, not an ion, kept.
- **S2 Q5 wording**, see above.
