# Figures — Chemistry pre-board 2016-17 (src/figs/chemistry_2016_17_figs.py)

All rendered with `preview.py` and at 2x zoom, and looked at. The paper prints no diagrams (one match table,
reproduced in HTML; the reaction scheme in 8(b) is typeset with mhchem), so all figures are answer figures.

- fig_cof6_cft (1(d)(i)): octahedral splitting with e_g ABOVE t2g; barycentre placed so e_g is +0.6Δo and t2g −0.4Δo (60 px / 40 px of a 100 px Δo). Co3+ d6 high spin: t2g ↑↓ ↑ ↑, e_g ↑ ↑ → 4 unpaired (option c).
- fig_fecl4_cft (5(b)): tetrahedral splitting reversed (e BELOW t2), drawn smaller than the octahedral one (Δt ≈ 4/9 Δo), e at −0.6Δt and t2 at +0.4Δt about the barycentre. Fe3+ d5: e ↑ ↑, t2 ↑ ↑ ↑ → 5 unpaired, μ = 5.92 BM.
- fig_ni_vbt (5(c)): Ni2+ 3d8 (↑↓ ↑↓ ↑↓ ↑ ↑); [NiCl4]2−: d unchanged, 4s + 3×4p take ligand pairs → sp3, 2 unpaired, paramagnetic; [Ni(CN)4]2−: four d pairs, the fifth d + 4s + two 4p take ligand pairs (one 4p left empty) → dsp2, 0 unpaired, diamagnetic. Brackets span exactly the hybridised boxes.
- fig_arrhenius_plot (4(b)(i)): log k vs 1/T, straight line of negative slope through scattered points, dashed extrapolation to the y-axis marked "intercept = log A", slope triangle Δ(1/T)/Δlog k, slope = −Ea/2.303R. Axes labelled with units.
- fig_amino_acid_forms (10(b)): cation H3N+–CHR–COOH (acid, → cathode), zwitter ion (isoelectric point, no migration), anion H2N–CHR–COO− (alkali, → anode); OH−/H+ on the equilibrium arrows point the right way (OH− forward towards the anion).
